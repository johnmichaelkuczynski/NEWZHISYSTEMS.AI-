🎯 Test Yourself
Stop re-reading. Start proving you know it.

Test Yourself turns any material — case files, textbooks, lecture notes, contracts, photos of a whiteboard — into a personal examination room. Upload what you need to master, tell the AI how to grill you, and get graded instantly with feedback that tells you exactly what to review next.

Built for people preparing for something that matters: a court hearing, a board exam, a certification, a defense, a deposition, an interview.

✨ Why Test Yourself?
Re-reading feels productive. It isn't. Decades of learning science agree on one thing: testing yourself is the fastest way to make knowledge stick. But writing your own practice questions is slow, and grading your own answers honestly is nearly impossible.

Test Yourself does both for you — relentlessly, instantly, and without flattery.

📚 Feed It Anything
Drop in your documents — PDFs, Word documents, and plain text files become quizzable material in seconds.
Snap a photo — pages, slides, whiteboards, handwritten notes. If a camera can see it, the app can read it and test you on it.
Paste it straight in — copied a passage, an email thread, or an outline? Paste and go.
Organize by project — keep your hearing prep separate from your licensing exam, each with its own materials, quizzes, and progress.
🧠 Quizzes That Work the Way You Study
You choose the format — any mix of multiple choice, short answer, and long answer, in whatever quantities you want.
You set the difficulty — warm up gently or go straight to the hard stuff.
You direct the examiner — tell the AI what to focus on in plain English: "Drill me on the timeline of events", "Only ask about the exhibits", "Ask the questions opposing counsel would ask."
You set the grading standard — lenient coach or merciless examiner. Your call: "Accept the gist" or "Dock me for every missed detail."
Fresh questions every time — no recycled question bank. Every quiz is generated from your material, on demand.
⚡ Instant, Honest Grading
Every answer scored on the spot — 0 to 100, the moment you submit.
Feedback that actually helps — what you got right, what you missed, and what to go back and review.
Model answers revealed — after you answer, see what a complete response looks like.
No waiting, no self-deception — the gap between "I think I know it" and "I can produce it under pressure" shows up immediately.
📈 Watch Yourself Get Sharper

Progress per project and overall — zoom into one subject or see the whole picture.
