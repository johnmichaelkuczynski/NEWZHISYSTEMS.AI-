📚 A Four-Week Journey Through the Sociological Perspective
No prior background required. Twenty-eight bite-size topics take you from your very first look at the sociological imagination to a working understanding of how societies hold together and change:

Week 1 — The Sociological Perspective — The sociological imagination · sociology vs. common sense · Durkheim, Marx, and Weber · functionalism, conflict theory, and symbolic interaction · the research process · surveys, experiments, and fieldwork · research ethics.
Week 2 — Culture, Self, and Society — Norms, values, and symbols · language and subcultures · socialization · the agents of socialization · the social construction of the self · everyday interaction · groups and bureaucracy.
Week 3 — Inequality and Social Difference — Social stratification · class and mobility · global stratification · race and ethnicity · prejudice and racism · sex and gender inequality · deviance and social control.
Week 4 — Social Institutions and Change — Family and marriage · education and religion · economy and work · power and the state · population, urbanization, and the environment · social movements · social change.
Every topic comes with lectures at three depths, conversation starters for your tutor, and unlimited adaptive practice.
