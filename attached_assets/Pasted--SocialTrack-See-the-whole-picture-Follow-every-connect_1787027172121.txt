🔍 SocialTrack
See the whole picture. Follow every connection.

SocialTrack is a visual investigation platform that turns scattered clues — usernames, emails, domains, phone numbers, crypto wallets — into a living map of connections you can explore, expand, and share.

Stop juggling twenty browser tabs and a spreadsheet. Drop what you know onto the canvas, and watch the picture come together.

✨ Why SocialTrack
Everything is a map — Investigations aren't lists, they're webs. SocialTrack shows people, accounts, websites, and wallets as an interactive graph, so relationships jump out at you instead of hiding in rows of data.
Start with almost nothing — One email address. One username. One domain. That's enough to begin — SocialTrack helps you branch outward from a single clue.
One-click expansion — Select anything on your map and enrich it: discover related domains, IP addresses, network ranges, crypto transactions, and more, added straight to your graph.
Built for real cases — Organize work into separate investigations, keep multiple sketches per case, and pick up exactly where you left off.
Team-ready — Share investigations with colleagues and work the same case together.
🚀 What You Can Do
Map a person's digital footprint — Connect usernames, emails, and social profiles into one clear picture.
Investigate websites & infrastructure — Trace domains to IP addresses, hosting networks, and related sites.
Follow the money — Explore crypto wallets, their transactions, and connected assets.
Design your own workflows — Chain discovery steps together into reusable flows, so your favorite investigation recipe runs with one click.
Ask the built-in assistant — Chat with an AI helper that understands your investigation and helps you decide what to check next.
Keep secrets safe — Store the API keys for your favorite data sources in a locked vault, encrypted end to end.
🧭 How It Works
Create an investigation — Give your case a name and open a fresh canvas.
Drop in a clue — Add a username, email, domain, phone number, or wallet address.
Expand — Run enrichments to discover connected information, automatically drawn onto your map.
Explore — Pan, zoom, rearrange, and inspect any node to see its full story.
Share — Invite teammates into the case or export your findings.
🎓 Designed For
Investigators & researchers verifying identities and mapping online activity
Journalists connecting sources, companies, and web infrastructure
Security teams tracing suspicious domains, IPs, and wallets
Anyone curious about how the pieces of the public internet fit together
🤝 Ethical by Design
SocialTrack is built for ethical investigation, transparency, and verification using publicly available information. Use it responsibly, respect privacy laws in your region, and only investigate what you're authorized to investigate.
