The Cult Member Is Not a Victim
There is, almost categorically, no such thing as a cult member who is a victim of the cult. The figure the therapeutic culture invites us to pity—the innocent soul preyed upon by a manipulative machine—does not survive contact with the actual structure of membership. To be a member is to be an agent: a cult exists only through the recruiting, policing, tithing, and proselytizing its members perform, so a purely passive member is not a member at all but dead weight, soon activated or extruded. The genuine victims of a cult are real and numerous—the children dragged along, the spouses drained, the friends recruited by trust and fleeced—but every one of them is a victim of a member, not a member themselves. The narrow exceptions, the woman born into a sealed sect and married off before she could read, the trafficked, the captured, belong to a separate class entirely: people stripped of agency and exit, who chose nothing and prey on no one. They are not cult members in the operative sense; they are the cult's livestock. Once you set them aside, the remaining members—the adults who walked in with the door open behind them—forfeit the word "victim" the moment they begin doing to others what was done to them.
What actually exists, in place of the victim, is the chump: the member who thought he would get something out of it and discovered he was the mark. This is a different and unsentimental category. The chump is not innocent, because his own appetite—for money, status, belonging, the promised killing—is precisely the lever the scheme uses against him, and his conversion is completed only when he turns that same lever on someone he loves. He is duped, but in this kind of structure the very content of being duped is the willingness to dupe; you do not fully belong until you have conned the next person in. So the broke, sweaty regional man reciting a prosperity he never received is not a tragic figure to be mourned but a chump to be diagnosed: an adult who lined up to do the hurting and happened to receive little or nothing for it. He was not done to. He was a volunteer in his own fleecing and a conscript of everyone he could reach. That is the honest taxonomy—not predator and victim within the membership, but chumps all the way down, each one paying for the privilege of victimizing the next.
