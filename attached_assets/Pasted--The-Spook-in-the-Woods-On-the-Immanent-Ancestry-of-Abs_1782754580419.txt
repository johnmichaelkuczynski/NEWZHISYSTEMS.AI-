**The Spook in the Woods: On the Immanent Ancestry of Abstract Objects**

The empiricist origin-myth casts so-called primitive man as a proto-Humean — a registrar of bare sensory atoms who chains pebble to gingerbread by brute habit and believes in nothing he cannot touch. The ethnographic record inverts this. Magical cognition is not impoverished but *over*-populated: it teems with hidden powers, witchcraft-substances, totemic natures, and the diffuse spirit-stuff of mana. The savage is no nominalist; he is an unrestrained essentialist, and empiricism is revealed as the late, disciplined achievement it is — an override of the native human setting, not its baseline. But his essences are not the transcendent abstracta of Plato. They are *immanent*: local, embodied, causally interactive, lurking in this cloud or that bone, bribable and woundable. They are extra-potent particulars rather than abstract universals — quasi-things with the edges sanded off, individuated enough to be fought and fed, abstract enough to be shared across instances and to persist when no instance is present. This is a middle tier between the dog over there and essence-of-dog, and it is best read not as a muddle between two categories the savage lacks but as the *undivided matrix* from which both later abstractions were eventually cleaved.

That this middle tier is the common ancestor of the disenchanted particular and the transcendent universal is what gives the picture its interest. Reabsorb the immanent power into its bearer and you get the bare dog; strip away its loose spatiotemporal coordinates and its causal grip, keeping only its shared-across-instances character, and you get essence-of-dog. The decisive evidence that this is metaphysics and not defective logic is the savage's own discrimination: he sees a flat contradiction in supposing that *Fido* — a bounded body subject to spatial exclusion — attacks two men a thousand miles apart at once, yet none whatever in the forest spook doing the same, because multiple-presence is constitutive of what such an effluence *is*. The principle of non-contradiction is intact and fires precisely where it should; only the inventory differs. The lesson, against Lévy-Bruhl's "prelogical mind," is that the inferential machinery never varied — what varied was the furniture of the world. The spirit that is wholly present in a thousand places without contradiction is the direct cognitive precursor of the property wholly present in a thousand apples: redness in the orchard is the forest spook, disciplined, abstracted, and finally lifted out of the woods.
