---
name: Approved Zhi promo style
description: The confirmed creative direction for future Zhi Systems promotional videos.
---

Zhi Systems promotional videos should be short, bright, visually light, and lightweight to upload. Use slow, natural conversational narration from a high-quality voice, real changing footage of the website, and prominently show and say ZhiSystems.ai. Clearly explain that displayed apps are examples of custom apps Zhi Systems has created, not a fixed product catalog. Static screenshot treatments, repeated backgrounds, rushed copy, and generic system voices are unacceptable even when basic pans or overlays are added.

**Why:** The owner explicitly confirmed the broader direction after rejecting a darker, abstract version, then rejected a later cut because the narration was rushed and robotic and the visuals felt like one repeated still image.

**How to apply:** Use this direction by default for future Zhi Systems promo videos unless the owner asks for a different format or style.
