---
name: Approved Zhi promo style
description: The confirmed creative direction for future Zhi Systems promotional videos.
---

Zhi Systems promotional videos should be short, bright, visually light, and lightweight to upload. Use natural conversational narration, real footage of the website, and prominently show and say ZhiSystems.ai. Clearly explain that displayed apps are examples of custom apps Zhi Systems has created, not a fixed product catalog.

**Why:** The owner explicitly confirmed this approach was perfect after rejecting a darker, abstract version with robotic narration.

**How to apply:** Use this direction by default for future Zhi Systems promo videos unless the owner asks for a different format or style.
